# Three-minute pitch

**Problem:** Seniors leave unused essentials while juniors buy the same items new; group-chat deals are scattered and prices uncertain.

**Solution:** GREEN offers a campus marketplace with an explainable price suggestion and search. A seller can choose a swap listing preference.

**Demo:** Enter a scientific calculator, good condition, 2 years old, bought for ₹1,200. Show the recommendation, explain it is calculated from synthetic comparables, publish it, then find it via search and filters.

**Insight:** Run `python insights.py` and quote a printed comparison including sample sizes. Clearly say these results reflect the assumptions used to generate the dataset; they are not observed market trends.

**AI and next steps:** Show the actual prompt in PROMPT_LOG.md and any mistakes genuinely observed. The next step is collecting consented, verified campus sales to evaluate the price model, followed by student verification and messaging.
