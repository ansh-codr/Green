# Three-minute pitch

**Problem:** Seniors leave unused essentials while juniors buy the same items new; group-chat deals are scattered and prices uncertain.

**Solution:** GREEN offers a campus marketplace with an explainable price suggestion and search. A seller can select a swap preference.

**Demo:** Enter a scientific calculator, good condition, 2 years old, bought for ₹1,200. Show the recommendation, explain it uses fictional comparables, publish it, then find it via search and filters.

**Insight:** For a trustworthy presentation, calculate a comparison from `data/past_listings.csv` and quote the sample sizes. Clearly state that these results reflect assumptions used to generate the dataset, not observed market trends.

**AI and next steps:** Show the actual prompt in PROMPT_LOG.md and any mistakes genuinely observed. Next, collect consented, verified campus sales to evaluate the price model, then add student verification and messaging.
